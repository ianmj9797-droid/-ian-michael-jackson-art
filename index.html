ABOUT THE ARTIST

Colorful, stylized realism.

Ian Michael Jackson is a Denver-based artist whose work centers on sports figures, emotional moments and the human form. Drawing on years of figure-drawing practice and fine-art study, he reimagines familiar subjects through color, simplification and expressive mark-making.

His work combines nostalgia with observation—inviting the viewer to recognize the moment, then look again.
